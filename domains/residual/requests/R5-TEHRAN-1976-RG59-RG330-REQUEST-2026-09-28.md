# R5 Tehran 1976 — RG59 / RG330 item-level request

## Goal
Locate item-level U.S. diplomatic/advisory/attaché records created around 19–20 September 1976 that could add maintenance, ground-radar, communications, or aircraft-system evidence beyond the already public DIA/JCS narrative message.

## Suggested recipient
National Archives at College Park / Archives II Reference Section (`archives2reference@nara.gov`) or the current NARA reference-inquiry channel for RG59/RG330.

## Request text

I am researching the U.S. archival provenance of the 19 September 1976 Iranian F-4 interception reported by USDAO Tehran and forwarded in a DoD/JCS message now available through the DIA Electronic Reading Room. I am not asking for an interpretation of the incident; I am trying to identify additional contemporaneous U.S. records and exact archival locations.

Could you please search or advise on item-level records for 19–20 September 1976 using the following identifiers and terms:

- Tehran / Mehrabad
- Imperial Iranian Air Force / IIAF
- F-4 / Phantom
- Shahrokhi Air Base
- USDAO Tehran / Defense Attaché
- ARMISH/MAAG
- radar / ground radar / radar lock
- maintenance / fault / instrumentation / communications failure

The public DIA/JCS message states that USDAO Tehran forwarded the event report and records an F-4 radar lock at 27 NM, a reported closure rate of 150 NMPH, an approximately maintained 25 NM range, and reported instrumentation/communications failures. I am specifically looking for records that could independently support, qualify, or falsify those reported measurements.

Archive anchors:

1. Record Group 59, Central Foreign Policy File, 1973–1979 (electronic telegrams and P-Reel index system; NARA identifies this series as the main State Department central recordkeeping system for the period).
2. Record Group 330, Near East and South Asia – Iran Country Files, 1972–1979, accession TR-0330-2020-0097 / W330-81-0223.

If available, please identify:

- exact telegram/message identifiers or P-Reel citations for 19–20 September 1976 related to this incident;
- embassy, USDAO, ARMISH/MAAG, or Defense/State follow-up correspondence;
- aircraft maintenance/fault reports or advisory records concerning the two F-4s;
- Mehrabad or other ground-radar records/plots, if transferred to U.S. custody;
- communications logs or attaché reporting with timestamps;
- exact file-unit/item titles, NAIDs, box/reel/frame references, and copy-order instructions.

Please also advise if the relevant maintenance/radar records would normally have remained solely in Iranian custody rather than entering U.S. archival holdings.

Thank you.

## Scientific gate
Success = item-level contemporaneous record that improves sensor/maintenance/timing evidence or an authoritative custody/disposition answer for the missing data class.

Not success = later witness testimony or another narrative summary without new measurements.
