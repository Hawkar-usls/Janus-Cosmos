# R5 RB-47 — NARA item/file-unit bind request

## Goal
Obtain an official NARA item/file-unit bind for the 17 July 1957 RB-47 event before treating any nonofficial case number or index locator as canonical.

## Suggested recipient
National Archives at College Park / Archives II Reference Section (`archives2reference@nara.gov`) or the current NARA reference-inquiry channel for Air Force textual records.

## Request text

I am researching the 17 July 1957 U.S. Air Force RB-47 incident for a source-provenance study. I am not requesting an interpretive determination about the event. I am trying to identify the exact archival object(s) and digital/copy-order path.

Could you please identify the exact NARA file-unit and/or item identifier(s) for records concerning the RB-47 event of 17 July 1957, including any Project Blue Book and Office of Special Investigations cross-references?

Stable public series anchors I am using are:

- Project Blue Book Case Files on Sightings of Unidentified Flying Objects, June 1947–December 1969 — NARA series/NAID 595466.
- Sanitized Version of Project Blue Book Case Files on Sightings of Unidentified Flying Objects, 1947–1969 — NARA series/NAID 597821.

A nonofficial discovery index points to “Project Blue Book case 4810” and “NARA-PBB1-167” for the 17 July 1957 event. I am treating those only as locator hints and would like NARA confirmation or correction rather than assuming they are canonical.

Specifically, if possible, please provide:

1. the exact file-unit/item NAID(s), title(s), date(s), and series;
2. box, roll, frame/page, or other physical/microfilm locator;
3. any digitized-object URL(s) or copy-order instructions;
4. any cross-reference to OSI, Air Defense Command, 745th ACWRON/Duncanville, or other Air Force investigative records;
5. whether the holdings include original sensor media/plots/recordings, or only reports/summaries describing sensor observations.

The key date is 17 July 1957. Location terms that may help include Gulf Coast / Mississippi / Louisiana / Texas / Duncanville, but I do not want those terms to override the date/event identity if the archival title uses different wording.

Thank you.

## Scientific gate
Success = official NARA item/file-unit bind or an official response establishing where the relevant records reside and what data classes survive.

Not success = another secondary reconstruction, witness retelling, or nonofficial case-number assertion.
