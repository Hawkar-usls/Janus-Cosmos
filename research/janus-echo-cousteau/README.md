# Janus-Echo-Кусто / Janus-Echo-Cousteau

Research branch honoring Jacques-Yves Cousteau.

Operational principle: use acoustics to detect and discriminate deep-sea structure without moving the frozen LOVE–EDEM target point or promoting morphology before direct evidence.

Historical anchor: J. Alinat & J.-Y. Cousteau (1962), “Accidents de terrain en mer de Ligurie”, in *Océanographie géologique et géophysique de la Méditerranée occidentale*, CNRS, pp. 121–123. Later marine-geology literature records this work as an early interpretation of small Ligurian abyssal-plain knolls as salt diapirs.

Current gate: `BLIND_REFERENCE_LIBRARY_V1`.

Scientific boundary: synthetic calibration is a software/inverse-problem calibration only. It is not a measured hydrophone library and is not evidence for an underwater pyramid.
