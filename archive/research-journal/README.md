## The research path in one page

### 1. Lave → LOVE

The mapped Lave point became blind `TARGET_A`. Gaia returned a ranked real-star neighborhood; HST/LUCI did not provide WCS-bound FITS for the frozen first pass; seven TESS sources were available. A blind TESS/BLS pass recovered a repeated ~7.749-day signal before identity unblinding. The authoritative unblind matched it to **TOI 3494.01**. This was therefore **not a new planet**, but it became a valuable positive control: the blind pipeline could recover a real already-catalogued signal without knowing its identity first. `Love` remained sealed.

### 2. LOVE → EDEM

LOVE and the EDEM candidate coordinates were resolved against AllWISE, 2MASS, SDSS, Pan-STARRS and a Gaia mirror. The sky centers are not empty, but no nearby catalog object was promoted to the identity of LOVE or EDEM. Direct Gaia service failures were treated as service failures, never as zero-source results.

### 3. LOVE × EDEM geometry

The pair is separated by about **90.5868°**. The geometry is real; its physical meaning is not established. The Orion Belt comparison remained post-hoc geometry only. No direct `sqrt(pi)` relation survived the numerical check.

A deterministic internal bisector model yields declination about **−3.865418°**. Forcing that bisector to local zenith fixes latitude but not longitude. Only after applying an inherited, already-frozen timestamp did the model produce the open-ocean point near **3.865418°S, 3.854924°E**. This is a mathematical Earth-locus construction, not evidence for a relay, structure, or ancient technology.

### 4. Frozen Africa / Atlantic point

GEBCO/GMRT infrastructure and broad Congo/Angola Basin acoustic/geophysical survey coverage were found, but exact direct-measurement provenance at the frozen point remained incomplete. The rule was therefore: **no recentering, no nearby-feature substitution, no morphology claim before direct-data provenance**.

The reverse-acoustic closure logic passed as a formal operator audit, but empirical identifiability remained blocked. The field state remained `NO_TARGET_DETECTED`.

### 5. Cousteau

Cousteau grew into a falsification-oriented ocean/acoustic lane: known-target controls, global-ocean and historical acoustic corpora, Atlantic-wide passes, T-phase catalogs, raw-data acquisition, 5D spiral / Tranception+Reverse passes, and explicit separation of measurement from interpretation.

The 117–121 Hz work eventually reached a predeclared response-corrected HA10 confirmatory test. The **119-Hz result is frozen negative**. Later controls cannot retroactively rescue it.

### 6. HA10 / H10N1

Native BAS Ascension bathymetry was recovered. At H10N1 the nearest native grid cell gives seafloor topography around **−1927.934326 m**. This is bathymetry, not hydrophone sensor depth.

Blind morphology controls were frozen before scoring. The preregistered primary 2-km result ranked **4/21, p≈0.1905**; a secondary 1-km result ranked **1/21, p≈0.0476**. Because the primary scale did not reproduce the local secondary result, the physical-anomaly gate did **not** advance. The 1-km result survives only as a watchlist item. No pyramidality or crater score was introduced afterward.

### 7. Southern Seamount / P2548

The Southern Seamount remains best supported as a known volcanic feature. Published descriptions include a flat/elongated summit morphology, central crags and a south-slope “wishbone,” but the exact wishbone coordinates have not been recovered. Secondary imagery was not authorized for quantitative digitization.

The 2002 Thales GeoSolutions Pacific survey lineage was reconstructed and the missing report **P2548 — Survey Report for HDAS Installation at Ascension Island, August 2002** identified. Public primary report bytes are still missing. Custodian lines are open with Fugro, CTBTO and NAVFAC.

### 8. JR15001 / BODC

BODC/NOC provided a crucial provenance correction: the originator `em122.ACO` contained multibeam depth channel `MBANSWCB`, but that channel was not transferred to final processed series 1762365; single-beam depth was retained because it was judged cleaner/more accurate in final screening.

We requested `em122.ACO` and associated originator navigation/provenance. The killer gate is frozen:

`HASH BYTES → VERIFY FILE/NAV → RECONSTRUCT ACTUAL EM122 SWATH → H10N1 IN/OUT → STOP → ASK JANUS`

Cruise proximity alone does not count as swath intersection.

### 9. TOBI CD169

The requested archival target is RRS Charles Darwin **CD169**, JD059, 2005-02-28, TOBI-02, science-log line 19, 01:00–01:29 UTC. BODC/NOC said an offline tape could be retrieved but wrote `CD196`; the public record strongly favors CD169, so we asked for custodian clarification before treating any delivered bytes as the requested dataset.

Before primary bytes arrive, JANUS authorized a synthetic-only geometry modulation. The log-derived closest approach around `01:07:25` is explicitly **calibration**, not an independent prediction. No target-interval TOBI pixels/traces have been used, and retuning after primary bytes arrive is forbidden.
