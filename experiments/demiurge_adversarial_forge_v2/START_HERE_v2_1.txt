JANUS COSMOS v2.1.0
===================

and repairs the admission protocol without retraining the frozen detector.
Targets must now outperform complete 20-field real-control cohorts. Orion also
requires random-position/random-orientation corridor specificity and actual
cross-survey morphology agreement. HST uses common-valid-support WF3 products
instead of the mosaic footprint.

1. Extract the ZIP into a NEW folder.
2. Double-click: run_janus_cosmos_v2_1.bat
3. Let the certificate checks, downloads and real-control run finish.
4. Send back the entire results_v2_1 folder, especially:
   - janus-cosmos-v2.1-report.json
   - janus-cosmos-v2.1-events.jsonl
   - terminal_v2.1.log
   - SUMMARY_v2.1.txt

Do not edit FROZEN_DETECTOR_v2_0.json, SPECIFICITY_PROTOCOL_v2_1.json or either
EXPECTED file. The runner verifies every identity and fails closed on drift.
