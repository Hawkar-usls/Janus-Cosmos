JANUS COSMOS v2.0.2
===================

This patch replaces the failed v2.0.1 decimal-rounding workaround with a
portable detector identity plus a separately hashed numerical evidence receipt.
Windows/Python/NumPy/SciPy differences are allowed only inside the registered
2e-6 synthetic-metric tolerance. Integrity checks remain fail-closed.

1. Extract the ZIP into a NEW folder.
2. Double-click: run_janus_cosmos_v2_0.bat
3. Let the forge, downloads and powered sky run finish.
4. Send back the entire results_v2_0 folder, especially:
   - janus-cosmos-v2.0-report.json
   - janus-cosmos-v2.0-events.jsonl
   - terminal.log
   - SUMMARY_v2.0.txt

Do not edit FROZEN_DETECTOR_v2_0.json or EXPECTED_FORGE_v2_0.json between the
forge and sky run. The runner verifies their hashes and fails closed on drift.
