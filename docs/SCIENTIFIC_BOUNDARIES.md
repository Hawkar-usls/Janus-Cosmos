- `P_VS_NP = OPEN`; neither `P=NP` nor `P!=NP` is established.
- No arbitrary-CNF bounded-separator theorem is established.
- No polynomial arbitrary-CNF SAT algorithm is established.
- Restricted/fixed finite compression is not proof of `P=NP`.
- One exponential representation or difficult family is not proof of `P!=NP`.
- SAT/UNSAT authority requires independently verifiable certificates; budget exhaustion is `UNKNOWN_BUDGET`.
- Preprocessing, discovery, verification, provenance and negative controls are charged.
- Failed preregistered runs remain failed; later improvements do not retroactively rescue them.
- `S𓂸ḥ` is a modern JANUS/OSIRIS alias/overlay, not a historical Egyptian spelling.
- `Sꜣḥ_ORION != sꜣḫ_TRANSFIGURATION != S𓂸ḥ_PROJECT_OVERLAY`.
- `ANCIENT_TEXT != MODERN_ALGORITHM`; `STRUCTURAL_PARALLEL != HISTORICAL_INTENT`.
