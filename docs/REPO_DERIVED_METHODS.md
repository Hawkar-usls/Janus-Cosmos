# Repo-derived methods for Janus Cosmos

This document records which reusable engineering patterns were inspected in the public Hawkar-usls repositories and which were integrated into Janus Cosmos.

## Integrated

### JANUS Fundamentum

Adopted the discipline of hypothesis -> attack -> survivor, explicit negative results, replayable evidence, and the rule that survival is not proof. Janus Cosmos now emits explicit gate rejection records and preserves them in the protocol graph.

### JANUS Lapis

Adopted candidate gates, rejection reasons, machine-readable ranking/decision records, and review-oriented summaries. A candidate is not merely a score; it carries an evidence hash and a gate decision.

### HRain

Adopted a graph-oriented observation model. Per-target/per-filter/per-seed observations become nodes, while seed replication and gate outcomes become explicit edges.

### JANUS Distributed AI Swarm

Adopted observer-first boundaries and immutable analysis invariants. The blind gate explicitly carries OCR/face/semantic/cipher/post-hoc/human-label restrictions, and experiment code must not reinterpret those invariants.

### Janus Demiurge

Adopted experiment-memory/provenance ideas only: run lineage, deterministic seed histories, and persistent experiment summaries. Adaptive optimization of detector thresholds is explicitly **not** imported into the blind scientific gate because it would create post-hoc tuning leakage.

## New geometric mechanics enabled by these patterns

The expanded runner now records:

- multi-threshold connected-component structure;
- rotational agreement at 45/90/135/180 degrees;
- radial Fourier-power distribution and entropy;
- smooth-vs-high-frequency energy separation;
- per-observation feature fingerprints;
- per-gate evidence hashes;
- seed replication graph;
- negative/rejected decisions as first-class data.

These are geometric descriptors only. They do not infer faces, text, language, objects, or human semantic labels.

## Non-integrated items

The following were deliberately not imported into the scientific gate: LLM suggestion layers, adaptive hyperparameter optimization, prediction/precognition semantics, narrative/world simulation, and any mechanism that could tune the detector after seeing the blind sample results.

## Claim boundary

A surviving candidate is an **image-level geometric anomaly candidate**. It is not an astronomical discovery until independent replication, scientific controls, and domain review establish that interpretation.
