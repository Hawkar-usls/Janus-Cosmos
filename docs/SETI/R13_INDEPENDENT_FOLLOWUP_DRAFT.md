## Unsent technical inquiry (not an observing proposal)
 
Subject: Feasibility of independent narrowband follow-up of archival Parkes feature toward HIP 47103
 
Dear Berkeley SETI Research Center,
 
We are independently reanalysing public Breakthrough Listen spectra as part of JANUS COSMOS. A weakly drifting feature around 3149.978159 MHz passes a preliminary same-session directional screen in Parkes observations on MJD 57782.623981 (2017). It occurs in three ON scans, with no strong matching track in two alternating OFF scans. A separate OFF-only feature approximately 165 Hz away and reverse-cadence control passes are substantial interference concerns. The same feature has not been recovered on earlier/later tested days under fixed topocentric or constant barycentric frequency hypotheses. We make no claim of extraterrestrial origin.
 
Reproducible R11/R12 receipts:
https://github.com/Hawkar-usls/Janus-Cosmos/actions/runs/44073590539

Original archived pointing: RA 9.600444444 h, Dec -20.339444444 deg; actual current pointing should be resolved from HIP47103 / L 678-39 with current astrometry and proper motion, not these old header values. Approximate reference barycentric frequency under constant-frequency hypothesis 3149.865052 MHz (R12); original topocentric drift +0.010204 Hz/s.
 
Would you advise whether existing high-resolution MeerKAT/ATA data cover this target and band, and whether an independently scheduled observational feasibility check is reasonable? A proposed minimal protocol would use calibrated narrowband dynamic spectra around 3149.865 MHz barycentric, plus a sufficiently wide topocentric window (e.g. >=1 MHz around 3.15 GHz after epoch/site correction), <=3 Hz channels, <=18 s integrations, alternate at least three ON and three matched OFF measurements of ~5 min each, and perform repeat observations on another date. Include exact timing, pointing, receiver calibration, interference log, and data provenance. If possible retain relevant voltage products or independently formed beams. The fixed-frequency hypotheses, decision rule, sensitivity and interference controls should be precommitted. No request for urgent or exceptional observing time is made.
 
We welcome a skeptical technical review and negative results. Please let us know the appropriate public archive or proposal/collaboration route.
